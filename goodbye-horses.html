Growing up is just getting taller

Goodbye Horses

Video | ART 113 Shana Moulton | 2026 | Robert Giron

Yes like the song name. A short documentary which mixes surrealism and non-linear narrative to explore themes of identity, transition, and the human experience. The project utilizes a mix of cam-corder footage taken over the last weeks of college, digital effects on CapCut, and abstract imagery to create a dreamlike atmosphere. It serves as a raw and unapologetic reflection of youth culture in the post-digital age.
Robert Giron 2026
