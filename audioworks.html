Audio Works '24-'26

Audio | MUS 109LB João Pedro Oliveira, MAT 111AU Jazer Sibley-Shwartz, Independent | 2024-2026 | Robert Giron

God said let there be noise. A collection of audio works created over the course of two years, this project showcases selected works from ventures into a variety of styles and techniques in sound design and music production. The works were created using a variety of software tools, including FL Studio, Ableton Live, as well as various virtual synthesizers such as Serum 2 and Vital.
