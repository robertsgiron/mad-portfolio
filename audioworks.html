Audio Works '24-'26

Audio | MUS 109LB João Pedro Oliveira, MAT 111AU Jazer Sibley-Shwartz, Independent | 2024-2026 | Robert Giron

God said let there be noise. A collection of audio works created over the course of two years, this project showcases selected works from ventures into a variety of styles and techniques in sound design and music production. The works were created using software tools such as FL Studio, Ableton Live, as well as virtual synthesizers such as Serum 2 and Vital.
