Background

Robert Giron is a multimedia artist and scholar exploring the intersection of culture and commerce. Born in Van Nuys, CA, of Salvadoran descent, Giron's background spans commercial client work, exhibition installation, and coursework in economics and media arts. His work bridges the gap between two worlds: digital and human. Regardless of scale, his practice investigates the “in-between”—the unseen forces that bind us together and generate meaning. In an era of rapid production, relentless consumption, and social change, Giron captures the essence of human identity, urging us to pursue life with reverence and choice.
